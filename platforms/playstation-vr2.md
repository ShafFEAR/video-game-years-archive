### Climate Station

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/coa0fj.jpg" alt="Climate Station cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** PlayStation 5, PlayStation VR2 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Simulator, Adventure

> Designed for the power of PlayStation 5 consoles, and enhanced for PS VR2, this free, immersive experience puts you in the middle of Earth’s most critical story: our own.  Over 90 minutes of interactive content - narrated by climate advocate Laura Tobin - takes you through rising seas, shifting temperatures, and stark climate futures, all built from real-world data by NASA, NOAA, and the IPCC.  Explore three main zones: Weather Year (a time-lapse of planetary change), Observations (where we are), and Projections (where we could go).
>
> _Synopsis source: IGDB_

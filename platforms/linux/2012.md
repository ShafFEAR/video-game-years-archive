### FTL: Faster Than Light

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co9chw.jpg" alt="FTL: Faster Than Light cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** Windows, Linux, macOS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Role-playing (RPG), Simulator, Strategy, Indie &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** September 14

> FTL: Faster Than Light is a single-player strategy game with roguelike design. The player commands a spaceship from a top-down view, managing systems such as weapons, engines, shields, and life support while recruiting crew and upgrading the vessel. The ship travels across procedurally generated sectors, jumping between beacons that present random events, stores, and enemy encounters. Combat plays out in real time with the option to pause and issue orders. Losing the ship or its crew ends the run and requires starting over, giving each playthrough a different layout and set of challenges.
>
> _Synopsis source: IGDB_

> The Cat Lady is a single-player horror graphic adventure and the second entry in the Devil Came Through Here trilogy, following Downfall and preceding Lorelai. It is a puzzle-based adventure played entirely with the four arrow keys, so characters move left and right between scenes while the up and down keys handle interacting with the environment and inventory. Dialogue is fully voiced, and occasional dialogue choices can shift the story and its ending slightly. The game deals with mature themes including depression, suicide, murder, and illness, alongside moments of humor.
>

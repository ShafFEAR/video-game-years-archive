### Klax

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co5ntg.jpg" alt="Klax cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** Arcade, Amiga, Atari ST, Amstrad CPC, BBC Micro, Commodore 64, Commodore 128, MS-DOS, MSX, SAM Coupé, ZX Spectrum, Nintendo Entertainment System, Atari 2600, PC Engine, Genesis / Mega Drive, Atari Lynx, Game Boy, Sharp X68000, PC-8800 series, PC-9800 series &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Developer:** Atari Games &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Publisher:** Atari Games, Namco, Domark, Tengen &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Puzzle &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** February 1990

> Klax is a 1989 computer puzzle game designed by Dave Akers and Mark Stephen Pierce. The object is to line up colored blocks into rows of similar colors to make them disappear, to which the object of Columns is similar. Atari Games originally released it as a coin-op follow up to Tetris, about which they were tangled in a legal dispute at the time.

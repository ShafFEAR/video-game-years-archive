### City Life

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/n6tkbwg32m4usqvvq0bz.jpg" alt="City Life cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** Windows, Nintendo DS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Developer:** Monte Cristo, Wizarbox &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Publisher:** Monte Cristo, Deep Silver, CDV &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** City building games &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** April 14, 2006

> City Life is another take on the old city building genre. Like SimCity, it requires you to build a functioning metropolis. However, City Life also adds a social aspect to the game. Your city is populated by citizens of six different economic classifications, from "Have-Nots" to "Blue Collars" to "Elites". Each social stratus has its own needs, some of which conflict with the needs of the other social classes. So you have to balance the social needs of your citizens as well as the zoning needs of your city. You have to adapt your strategies according to the citizens' needs, who communicate with you.  A large amount of detailed structures is available, both commercial and residential. There are 22 maps with different goals, but an editor has been included to design custom maps, or you can start in the sandbox mode. Custom maps and scenarios can be exported and shared with other players. City Life is powered by a full 3D engine, allowing for full rotation and zooming as if you are walking in your own streets.
>
> _Synopsis source: IGDB_

### Digimon World DS

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co2vtu.jpg" alt="Digimon World DS cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** Nintendo DS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Developer:** BEC &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Publisher:** Bandai Namco Games &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Role-playing video game &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** June 15, 2006

> Digimon World DS, known in Japan as Digimon Story (デジモンストーリー Dejimon Sutōrī?), is a role-playing video game and the first Digimon game for the Nintendo DS. It is not part of the Digimon World series, as suggested by its localized title, but the game does bears a resemblance to Digimon World 3.  In the game, the player assumes the role of a Digimon tamer and embarks on a journey to discover, tame, raise, train and command more than 230 unique Digimon. The player can build Digi-Farms to raise, evolve and communicate with the Digimon. Using Wi-Fi and local DS wireless connection, players can interact by exchanging Digimon, engaging in battles, and pooling resources to create rare types of Digimon.  To date, the game has spawned four sequels in Digimon World Dawn and Dusk, Digimon Story Lost Evolution, Digimon Story: Super Xros Wars Red and Blue, and Digimon Story: Cyber Sleuth.
>

> Caesar II is set in the time of ancient Rome, and lets you try to be the next Caesar. You start with one province and have to build an efficient city. To do this you'll have to manage the water supply properly, make sure the city is policed well, and provide adequate education, entertainment, baths, and temples (which also hold your money and can be robbed if you don't have enough protection). ### Discworld

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co4z82.jpg" alt="Discworld cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** MS-DOS, macOS, PlayStation &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Developer/Publisher:** Psygnosis &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Point-and-click, Puzzle, Adventure &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** March 17

> Discworld is a game released for PC (subsequently Mac, and Playstation) by Teeny Weeny / Perfect 10 studios in 1995. The game was released both on floppy disk and CD-Rom. The latter featured a commentary team created of many veterans of famous Brit-Coms. This included Monty Python's Eric Idle, Radio 4's (later to be more famous) Rob Brydon, ex- Doctor Who John Pertwee, 70s pop singer Kate Robbins, and Blackadder star Tony Robinson. The game is a riff on the Discworld title 'Guards, Guards', however many elements of the text have been shifted, not least the inclusion of Rincewind as protagonist.  The game featured puzzles which were considered far too difficult and obtuse for gamers of the time, but the game itself has had a lasting legacy, now being accepted into such emulators as SCUMMVM.
>

### I Have No Mouth, and I Must Scream

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co9jsr.jpg" alt="I Have No Mouth, and I Must Scream cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** MS-DOS, macOS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Developer/Publisher:** Cyberdreams &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Adventure &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** October 31

> I Have No Mouth, and I Must Scream is a point-and-click adventure game based upon Harlan Ellison's short story of the same title. The game's story is set in a world where an evil computer named AM has destroyed all of humanity except for five people, whom he has been keeping alive and torturing for the past 109 years. Each survivor has a fatal flaw in their character, and in an attempt to crush their spirits, AM has constructed a metaphorical adventure for each that preys upon their weaknesses. To succeed in the game, the player must make choices to prove that humans are better than machines, because they have the ability to redeem themselves. Woven into the fabric of the story are ethical dilemmas dealing with issues such as insanity, rape, paranoia and genocide.
>

> Marathon 2: Durandal is the sequel to the first Marathon.

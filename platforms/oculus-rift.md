### Phasmophobia

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/coamcz.jpg" alt="Phasmophobia cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** SteamVR, Windows, Windows Mixed Reality, Oculus Rift &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Developer:** Kinetic Games &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Publisher:** Kinetic Games &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Puzzle, Tactical, Indie &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** September 18, 2020

> Phasmophobia is a cooperative horror investigation game played from a first-person perspective, supporting one to four players. Players take on the role of ghost hunters who must identify the type of ghost haunting a designated location by gathering evidence using various paranormal investigation equipment. The game features speech recognition, allowing ghosts and certain equipment to respond to words spoken by players. As investigators spend time at the site, their sanity decreases, eventually triggering hunts during which the ghost actively pursues and can kill players. The game includes multiple location types of varying size, twenty-nine ghost types each with unique behaviors and evidence, and a progression system where players earn money to unlock and upgrade equipment. Virtual reality is optionally supported.
>

### Defense Grid 2

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co1ru7.jpg" alt="Defense Grid 2 cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** Xbox One, Linux, PlayStation 4, Oculus Rift, macOS, Windows &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Developer:** Hidden Path Entertainment &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Publisher:** 505 Games, Hidden Path Entertainment &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Real Time Strategy (RTS), Simulator, Strategy, Indie &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** September 23, 2014

> Defense Grid 2, DG2, is the highly-anticipated sequel to Hidden Path Entertainment’s 2008 Defense Grid: The Awakening. Setting the bar as the definitive tower defense game, Defense Grid 2 introduces new worlds and threats to test your tower placement strategies. With a bold new look, a compelling single-player campaign, and the addition of new game modes, online player-versus-player and multiplayer co-op, every play-through brings fun new opportunities and challenges.  DG2 comes with a story campaign featuring 21 stunning maps, dynamic level movement, an expanded story and cast of characters, hundreds of challenge mode experiences, new multiplayer modes, a procedurally driven audio score and the ability for players to build their own levels. DG2 also introduces players to DG Architect, a level creation tool set connected to Steam Workshop where players can create their own unique levels to share and possibly even sell if selected.  DG2 for the PC was funded in part by Dracogen, a private investor after a 2012 Kickstarter campaign funded the original game's DLC, Defense Grid: Containment. DG2 is developed by Hidden Path Entertainment and published by 505 Games.
>

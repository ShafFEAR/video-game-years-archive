### Fire Emblem: Thracia 776

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/cobafv.jpg" alt="Fire Emblem: Thracia 776 cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** Super Nintendo Entertainment System &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Developer:** Intelligent Systems &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Publisher:** Nintendo &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Tactical role-playing &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** September 1, 1999

> Fire Emblem: Thracia 776 is a Japanese tactical role-playing game developed by Intelligent Systems and published by Nintendo, and the fifth installment in the Fire Emblem series. It is also the third and final Fire Emblem series title to be released on the Super Famicom.  Fire Emblem: Thracia 776 takes place between Chapters 5 and 6 of the previous game, Fire Emblem: Seisen no Keifu. Several characters from Fire Emblem: Seisen no Keifu appear, such as Leaf, Fin, and Nanna. It takes place in the Thracian peninsula in southeastern Jugdral.

### Power Lode Runner

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co7ts3.jpg" alt="Power Lode Runner cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** Super Nintendo Entertainment System &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Puzzle

> Power Lode Runner is a Super Famicom video game published by Nintendo on the first day of 1999. The game is an updated version of Lode Runner. In the game you'll control Muguru-kun whose primary goal is to collect gold in order to progress through the game. Monkeys will act as the game's villains. The game was released as a Nintendo Power RAM cartridge.

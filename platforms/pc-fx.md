### All Japan Women's Pro Wrestling: Queen of Queens

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co1mz1.jpg" alt="All Japan Women's Pro Wrestling: Queen of Queens cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** PC-FX &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Developer:** HuneX Co. Ltd. &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Publisher:** NEC Home Entertainment Ltd. &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Wrestling, Fighting &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** March 24, 1995

> Queen of Queens is a wrestling game featuring licensed stars from All Japan Women's Pro-Wrestling, such as Aja Kong, Akira Hokuto, and others. The game follows the system introduced in Battle Heat, with every move shown as a full-motion video sequence. Unlike Battle Heat, however, Queen of Queens does not use graphics, but videos of real wrestlers.  There are two modes in the game: League and Free Battle. League Mode is based on the All Japan Women's Pro-Wrestling championship and follows the rules of the league. It is possible to play against the computer or in two-player mode.
>

### Langrisser II

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/coaxgj.jpg" alt="Langrisser II cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** Genesis / Mega Drive, Super Nintendo Entertainment System, Sega Saturn, PlayStation, PC-FX, Windows &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Developer:** Masaya Games &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Publisher:** Nippon Computer Systems &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Tactical role-playing game &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** August 26, 1994

> Langrisser II is a turn-based strategy game that was developed by Team Career, part of Masaya, and published by the parent company NCS Corporation.  This version of the game was released on the Nintendo Wii through the Virtual console and was later released for Windows though a classic games service.  Unlike the first Langrisser, known as Warsong internationally, almost all of the other games and remakes in the series have not been released outside of Japan.

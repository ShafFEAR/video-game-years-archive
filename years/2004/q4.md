### Alexander

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co1ywx.jpg" alt="Alexander cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** Windows &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Developer:** GSC Game World &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Publisher:** Ubisoft &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Real-time strategy &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** November 23, 2004

> Alexander is an RTS based on the movie of the same same, and the real-life Alexander the Great. The game follows the same general path as the movie, and includes media straight from the film. Present in the game are Greek, Persian, Indian and Egyptian forces, with a campaign for each. The hybrid 2D/3D engine (2D units, 3D terrain - also used in Cossacks 2: Napoleonic Wars) allows for a very large number of troops on the field. This is used to good effect, as battles can easily contain hundreds of units. Finally, multiplayer and skirmish levels are available, and the soundtrack (of the game and the movie) is by the composer Vangelis.
>
> _Synopsis source: IGDB_

The world is navigated using an overworld map using point-to-point navigation, with a date system showing the length of time it takes to get to a selected location. ### Zoo Tycoon 2

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co8z9n.jpg" alt="Zoo Tycoon 2 cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** Windows &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Developer:** Blue Fang Games, Rapan &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Publisher:** Microsoft Game Studios &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Business simulation &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** November 9, 2004

> Zoo Tycoon 2 is the sequel to Zoo Tycoon. The sequel updates the graphics, but keeps much of the original gameplay the same.  You start with an open field and must build it up into a zoo. Create exhibits and populate them with animals. Build amenities and concession stands to keep visitors happy in between exhibits. Happy visitors will donate more money to your zoo so you can make it better. However, you have much more freedom in exhibit creation. You don't even need fences; a moat or ditch will suffice, as this technique is popular at real high profile zoos. There are also more animals and attractions to place in your zoo, and animal habitats look as realistic as the cartoony graphics style will allow.

### Zoo Vet

<img src="https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/cobglz.jpg" alt="Zoo Vet cover" width="200">

**Platforms:** Windows &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Developer:** Legacy Games &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Publisher:** Legacy Games &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Genre:** Simulation &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Released:** December 1, 2004

> Lions, tigers and bears... oh, my! In this game you'll get up close and personal with wild animals, because you are one of their doctors! Assume the role of a zoo veterinarian where you can pet, talk to and treat all types of animals in need of help. Every type of animal imaginable is a guest at this world-class zoo and you'll have the opportunity to meet dozens of them in Zoo Vet.  There are several difficulty modes to choose from. You'll receive help along the way from zookeepers and other veterinarians on staff. Look up facts and play animal trivia to learn more about all of the animals. Earn up to 1,000 per case. The number of points determines what trophies and awards you win. If you do well enough, you will unlock more challenging cases and the praise of your co-workers. Win the game by getting a permanent job at the zoo.  Travel to each of the animal habitats to perform examinations and evaluate the problems. Use 40 medically-accurate tools - even perform surgeries when needed! Go back to your office to relax, play bonus mini-games or to do research.  The game features a simple point-and-click interface, rendered 3D graphics and voices by real actors and actresses. Zoo Vet is a family-oriented game for all ages.
